Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What We Use

DocForBro does not use traditional tracking cookies. Instead, we use your browser's localStorage to store essential preferences.

2. Essential Storage

  • Theme preference: We save whether you prefer Light or Dark mode so the app looks right when you return.
  • Session tokens: When you sign in, Supabase stores a secure token in your browser to keep you logged in.
  • Working drafts: In-progress notes, rate calculations, and margin projects are temporarily saved to your local browser before being synced to the cloud.

3. No Third-Party Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, or any third-party advertising trackers.
