Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create a DocForBro account, we collect your email address and basic authentication data via Supabase. Any documents, notes, contacts, or templates you create are stored securely in your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the DocForBro service. This includes saving your drafts, calculating rates, and generating PDFs. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to save your theme preference (light/dark mode) and any unsaved working drafts to prevent data loss if you refresh the page.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for authentication and database storage. We use html2canvas and jsPDF (loaded via CDN) to generate PDFs locally on your device. Your documents are never sent to an external server for PDF generation.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time from the Settings page. If you wish to delete your entire account, please contact us.
